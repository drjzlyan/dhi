Write for someone smart who was not in the room:

- Lead with what the thing IS and when to use it — one paragraph.
- Show one complete, runnable example before explaining options.
- Name the failure modes: what breaks, what it looks like, the fix.
- Prefer deleting stale text over adding caveats around it.
